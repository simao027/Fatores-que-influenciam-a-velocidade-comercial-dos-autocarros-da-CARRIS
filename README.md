# Fatores que Influenciam a Velocidade Comercial dos Autocarros na Cidade de Lisboa: A Empresa de Transportes Públicos Rodoviários CARRIS


In Lisbon, approximately one in every two trips made by the population is made by public
transport. Given the high demand for this type of service, it is essential to maintain high
standards of quality and efficiency. However, the average speed recorded by the CARRIS
fleet, the entity responsible for operating bus, tram and lift services in the city, was 13.54
km/h in 2023, a value that has shown a negative trend year after year, except during the
COVID-19 pandemic period.

A low speed, in addition to frustrating passengers, leads to economic losses for the
operator. During 2023, if there had been an increase of only 0.5 km/h in the average speed,
the fleet could have travelled an additional 1.2 million kilometres using the same
resources. Lower speeds also result in longer journey times and have a significant social
impact on the quality of life of Lisbon’s residents.
This research focused on understanding to what extent the Commercial Speed (VC) of
CARRIS buses is affected by external factors such as traffic conditions, meteorology and
the school calendar.

The adopted methodology was CRISP-DM, in which all six of its phases were applied.

Three datasets were used: operational data from CARRIS, meteorological data from
IPMA and traffic data from Waze. Exploratory and predictive analyses were carried out
to identify which factors most influence the daily performance of the fleet.

Finally, a dynamic dashboard was developed, allowing the selection of various
parameters (date, time interval, weather conditions, bus line and type of day). Based on
the selected filters, it generates different results, charts and an interactive map that
highlights the most problematic locations and lines, where the service operates at belowaverage speeds.

This study provides relevant information to improve the service and guide future work.
